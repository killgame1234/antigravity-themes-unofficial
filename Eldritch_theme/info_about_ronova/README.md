# Ronova (若娜瓦) — The Ruler of Death (Genshin Impact)
### Celestial Shade of Death • Arbiter of Heavenly Principles

This directory contains official art, cutscene cinematics, non-human eldritch celestial eye forms, and combat skill showcases for **Ronova**, the **Ruler of Death** in *Genshin Impact*.

---

## 🌌 Lore & Character Identity

- **True Title:** Ruler of Death (死之执政) / Shade of Death
- **Aliases:** Tuonetar (Frostmoon Scions), Arbiter of the Heavenly Principles
- **Japanese Voice Actress:** Fumiko Orikasa (折笠富美子)
- **Origin & Nature:** One of the **Four Great Rulers (Shades)** created by the Primordial One (Phanes) alongside Asmoday (Space) and Istaroth (Time). She governs the laws of life, death, and mortality across Teyvat.
- **Divine Powers:** Absolute authority to define how any entity meets its demise or to deny death entirely. Responsible for casting the curse of immortality upon the pure-blooded citizens of Khaenri'ah and establishing the rules of Natlan's Night Kingdom and the sacred Resurrection Chant.
- **Visual Motif:** Crimson/blood-red color palette, gothic black widow dress, arms marked with eye patterns, glowing winged appendages lined with multiple red eyes, and the ability to manifest as a colossal cosmic eye in the sky peering down upon reality.
