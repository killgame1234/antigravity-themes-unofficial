# Antigravity 2.0 Custom Themes

A collection of custom live-injected themes and interactive companions for **Google Antigravity 2.0 Desktop**.

## Included Themes

### 1. Cyberpunk: Edgerunners Season 2 — Capri (`Project Capricorn`)
- **Folder**: `Capri/`
- **Launcher**: `Capri/Apply_Capri_Theme.bat`
- **Features**:
  - Crimson-magenta (`#FF1E56`), toxic emerald-green (`#00E64D`), and void-violet (`#07030D`) theme matched to Capri's color palette.
  - **Cyberpsychosis Hover Glitch** & interactive electric lightning arcs across UI controls.
  - **Interactive Capri Companion**: Walks around the workspace perimeter, performs zero-G holographic afterimage poses, speaks characteristic lines on click, and occasionally strikes chat cards with high-voltage lightning.
  - **Upside-Down Ceiling Rift Pop-Out**: Periodically drops down upside-down from a glowing rift at the top of Antigravity (`0:00–0:06` animation).
  - **Right-Click Neural Command Console**: Toggle movement, toggle lightning attacks, trigger manual zaps or ceiling pop-outs, and chat directly with Capri.

### 2. Project Moon — The Index & The Prescripts
- **Folder**: `Prescript/`
- **Launcher**: `Prescript/Apply_Prescript_Theme.bat`
- **Features**:
  - Obsidian-black OLED void (`#020508`) with cyan (`#00E5FF`) & metallic gold (`#D4AF37`) Index aesthetics.
  - Animated **Strings of Fate** `<canvas>` background and floating alpha-keyed **Index Hand Emblem**.
  - **Dynamic Prescript Generator**: Typewrites a new canonical or procedural Index Prescript decree behind the emblem every 2 minutes.

### 3. Eldritch Void & Crimson Ocular Horror
- **Folder**: `Eldritch_theme/`
- **Launcher**: `Eldritch_theme/Apply_Eldritch_Theme.bat`
- **Features**:
  - Deep abyssal void & crimson ocular horror palette with a live video background (`eldritch_bg.mp4`).
  - Custom Eldritch Eye header rift and stop button.

## Usage
Double-click any theme's `.bat` launcher while **Antigravity 2.0** is open to inject or switch themes live without restarting.
